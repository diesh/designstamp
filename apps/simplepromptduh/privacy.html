Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

simple PROMPT DUH ("the app") is a free teleprompter tool. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

What the app accesses

The app has two ways to load a script. If you paste or type text directly, that text stays in your browser and no Google account is needed. If you choose to sign in with Google to load a Google Doc instead, the app requests read-only access to Google Drive (the drive.readonly scope), solely so it can fetch the plain-text content of the specific Doc you choose to load. The app cannot see, list, or modify any other files in your Drive.

What gets stored, and where

Nothing you do in this app is sent to or stored on any server I run. There is no backend, no database, and no account system beyond Google's own sign-in.

  • Pasted text and Google Doc text are held only in your browser's memory while you use the app.
  • Your document link, pasted text, and display preferences (font, speed, mirror settings, etc.) are saved using your browser's local storage, on your device only. I never receive this information.
  • Your Google sign-in token, if used, stays in your browser session to make the Drive API request, and is not transmitted anywhere else.

Third parties

The app uses Google's Identity Services and Drive API for sign-in and document access, subject to Google's own privacy policy. No other third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used.
